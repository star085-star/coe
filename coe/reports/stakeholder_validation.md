# Stakeholder Validation

> **STATUS: NOT YET DONE.** I could not interview real operators or engineers, and this file deliberately contains no invented quotes.

## 1. Protocol (15 min each)
1. Show the Patterns tab (EN or TA) and ask: "What would you fix first, and why?" Compare with the system's top pattern.
2. Give 5 stoppage cards and ask the participant to pick a cause, then compare with the system (agreement rate).
3. Ask them to log one stop with the Live-triage form in their preferred language (time-on-task, errors).
4. Show a VERIFIED and a NOT_EFFECTIVE action; ask whether they would trust it and what extra evidence they need.
5. Questionnaire (1-5): clarity, trust, effort, language comfort, fear of being monitored; free text: "what would make you stop using this?"

## 2. Success criteria (set before testing)
Agreement with system top-pattern >= 3 of 5 respondents; task completed unaided; trust >= 4 for engineers; **monitoring-concern <= 2 for operators** (otherwise redesign).
